# Profile choice data sources

- `india-states-districts.json` is sourced from the public Indian Government Information portal at https://igod.gov.in/sg/district/states and packaged from the dataset at https://github.com/KTBsomen/Indian-state-district-json. The dataset's MIT license and copyright notice are included in `INDIAN-STATE-DISTRICT-LICENSE.txt`.
- `kerala-communities.json` is based on the Kerala Public Service Commission community lists at https://www.keralapsc.gov.in/list-scheduled-castes-kerala-state. Community names are for member self-description only; they do not determine certificate or reservation eligibility. `Other / not listed` and `Prefer not to say` remain available.

These are snapshots. Update the location list when administrative boundaries change, and review Kerala Government community-list updates before refreshing the community choices.
