# Profile choice data sources

- `india-states-districts.json` is sourced from the public Indian Government Information portal at https://igod.gov.in/sg/district/states and packaged from the dataset at https://github.com/KTBsomen/Indian-state-district-json. The dataset's MIT license and copyright notice are included in `INDIAN-STATE-DISTRICT-LICENSE.txt`.
- `kerala-communities.json` contains community labels curated from the site owner's requested list. They are for member self-description only; they do not determine certificate or reservation eligibility. `Caste No Bar`, `Inter-Caste / No Caste`, `Other / not listed`, and `Prefer not to say` are included as selectable choices.

These are snapshots. Update the location list when administrative boundaries change, and review the community choices when the site owner requests changes.
